# TTS Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 4, 2026**

TTS Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord application that provides text-to-speech, automatic message reading, translation, voice playback, and related server features.

By using TTS Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

TTS Bot may process the following information when required to provide its features:

### Discord Information
- Discord User ID
- Discord Server ID
- Discord Channel ID
- Discord Voice Channel ID
- Server configuration and settings
- Bot permissions and connection information

### Message Content

If automatic message reading is enabled in a server, TTS Bot may process messages from the channel configured by the server administrators.

Message content is processed only to provide the requested text-to-speech or translation functionality.

### Voice and TTS Data

Text submitted for TTS may be processed by the Bot's text-to-speech system to generate audio.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by TTS Bot may be used to:

- Convert text into speech
- Read messages automatically when enabled
- Translate text when requested
- Play generated audio in voice channels
- Maintain server-specific settings
- Provide bot commands and features
- Diagnose technical problems and maintain reliability
- Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot

We do not use collected information for purposes unrelated to providing or maintaining TTS Bot.

## 3. Message Reading

TTS Bot includes an optional automatic message-reading feature.

Server administrators can configure a channel for automatic reading. When this feature is enabled, messages in the configured channel may be processed by the Bot.

Administrators can disable automatic reading using the available Bot commands.

TTS Bot does not require automatic message reading to be enabled in every server.

## 4. Data Storage

TTS Bot may store limited configuration information required for the Bot to operate correctly, such as:

- Server settings
- Configured channels
- TTS preferences
- Language or voice preferences
- Prefix or command settings

We do not intentionally store the full contents of every message sent in servers.

Temporary data may be processed while a command or TTS request is being completed.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally provide personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Some information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide a specific feature, such as text-to-speech or translation services.

Only information reasonably required for that feature should be sent to such services.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain configuration data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide TTS Bot's functionality.

Temporary TTS, translation, or playback data may be removed after it is no longer required.

If a server removes TTS Bot, server-specific configuration may be deleted according to our data-retention procedures.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by TTS Bot.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or highly personal information through the Bot.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TTS Bot is designed to operate as a Discord application and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

We do not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary for the Bot's functionality.

## 9. User and Server Requests

Server administrators or users may contact us regarding data processed by TTS Bot.

Requests may include questions about:

- Stored server settings
- Data deletion
- Privacy concerns
- TTS Bot's data processing

We may require sufficient information to verify the request before making changes or providing information.

## 10. Third-Party Services

TTS Bot may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including text-to-speech, translation, hosting, or other technical services.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms of service. TTS Bot is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

## 11. Discord

TTS Bot operates through Discord.

Your use of Discord and interaction with the Bot is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

TTS Bot does not control Discord's collection or processing of information.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TTS Bot's features, data processing practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published through the official TTS Bot repository or other official TTS Bot channels.

## 13. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or TTS Bot's data practices, contact the TTS Bot development team through the official support/contact method provided by TTS Bot.
